Privacy Policy

Units: Ruler & Vault · Last updated October 30, 2026 · iPhone and Android

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server and no analytics. Your photos, videos, files, notes and patterns stay on your phone, encrypted, and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. On Android, Google Play's billing library handles the purchase under Google's privacy policy.

Crash reports

If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They do not contain your vault contents. On Android, if you share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may show us anonymous crash and performance data in the same way.

Permissions

iPhone: camera and microphone are used only to take photos or videos inside a vault. Photo library access is used only when you ask the app to delete originals after importing them.

Android: the app asks for no permissions. Photos and videos are taken with your camera app straight into the vault, and imports use the system picker. The only network access comes from Google Play's billing library, used for purchases.

Backups

Vault data is excluded from iCloud, Google and device backups. Deleting the app deletes your vaults.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
