Privacy Policy

Units: Ruler & Secret Vault · Last updated October 29, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server and no analytics. Your photos, videos, files, notes and patterns stay on your phone, encrypted, and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Crash reports

If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They do not contain your vault contents.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are used only to take photos or videos inside a vault. Photo library access is used only when you ask the app to delete originals after importing them.

Backups

Vault data is excluded from iCloud and device backups. Deleting the app deletes your vaults.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
